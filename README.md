Simple Interest Calculator

1. Formula
Simple Interest (I) = P * r * t
Total Amount (A) = P + I
Where:
* P = Principal (the initial amount of money borrowed or invested)
* r = Annual Interest Rate (expressed as a decimal, e.g., 5% = 0.05)
* t = Time period (in years)


2. Key Variables
* Principal (P): Starting balance (e.g., $1,000)
* Rate (r): Percentage charged or earned per year (e.g., 5%)
* Time (t): Duration of the loan or investment in years (e.g., 3 years)


3. Example Calculation
* Principal (P): $1,000
* Rate (r): 5% per year (0.05)
* Time (t): 3 years


Interest (I) = 1000 * 0.05 * 3 = $150
Total Amount (A) = 1000 + 150 = $1,150
4. Characteristics
* Fixed Growth: Interest earned each year remains constant because it is only calculated on the original principal.
* Non-Compounding: Interest is not added back to the principal to earn additional interest in future periods.
* Common Uses: Short-term personal loans, auto loans, simple savings agreements, and basic bonds.
